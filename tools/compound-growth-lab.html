What to experiment with

Keep everything fixed and move only the years: 15, then 25, then 35. The jump between the last two is the compounding story in one gesture — growth on growth, not just more deposits. Next, halve the monthly contribution and notice how much less the ending moves than you feared; consistency beats size at long horizons.

Honest limits

This lab uses a smooth assumed return. Markets are not smooth. Use the output to compare habits, never as a promise about a balance.
