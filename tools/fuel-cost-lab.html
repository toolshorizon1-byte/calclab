What to experiment with

Lower the fuel economy by 10% — the realistic penalty for a loaded car at motorway speed — and see the honest version of the cost. Then add 10% to the distance for detours and in-town driving. Budgeting with both adjustments is the difference between an estimate and a surprise.

The idea

Fuel needed is distance divided by economy; cost is that fuel times price. Three honest inputs beat one optimistic guess.
