What to experiment with

Change the lowest grade up one letter and rerun. That single change moves the average more than raising an A ever could — which is why improvement plans should start with the weakest subject and with any missing assignments, whose zeros drag hardest.

The idea

Grades become points (A=4, B=3, C=2, D=1, F=0), points are averaged. Some schools weight advanced classes higher; this lab uses the standard unweighted scale and says so.
