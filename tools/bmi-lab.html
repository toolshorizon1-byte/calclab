What to experiment with

Move the weight by 3 kg either way and watch the index shift — near a range boundary, small changes flip the label, which is one reason not to over-read a single reading. Athletes and older adults in particular should pair this number with waist size and how they actually feel.

The idea

BMI compares weight with height squared. It was built for population statistics and works best as a first screen, not a personal verdict.
