What to experiment with

Change one variable at a time. Shortening the term from 60 to 48 months raises the payment slightly but usually cuts total interest sharply, because interest has fewer months to accumulate. Then try the rate: half a percentage point on a long loan is worth more than most people expect.

The idea

Each payment splits into interest on the current balance plus principal. Early payments are interest-heavy; later ones are principal-heavy. That split — not the payment size — is what changes over the life of the loan.
