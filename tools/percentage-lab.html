What to experiment with

Try a shop scenario: 15% off 240. Then a pay scenario: 240 increased by 15%. The same two numbers answer both, because a discount and a raise are the same operation wearing different signs. Finally, notice that a 50% loss needs a 100% gain to undo — run 100 decreased by 50%, then increase the result by 50% and see where you land.

The idea

Percent means per hundred. Every percentage problem is multiplication by a fraction with 100 underneath, once you see it.
