Small tools. Instant answers. Nothing uploaded.

CalcLab is a bench of six interactive calculators — loans, growth, BMI, GPA, percentages and fuel — each paired with a short experiment to try. Everything runs in your browser; the numbers you type never leave your device.

The labs

Borrowing

Loan Payment Lab

Type a loan, see the payment and total interest instantly — plus what one extra $50 a month does.

Saving

Compound Growth Lab

A slider-free growth illustrator: starting amount, monthly habit, assumed return, years. Watch time do the work.

Health

BMI Lab

Enter height and weight for an instant BMI and its standard adult range — with the caveats the number deserves.

School

GPA Lab

Paste letter grades in any order and get a standard 4.0 GPA instantly — built for quick mid-term checks.

Math

Percentage Lab

One tool for the three percentage questions of daily life: part of a number, an increase, and a decrease.

Travel

Fuel Cost Lab

Distance, real fuel economy, local price: an honest trip fuel estimate in five seconds.

Why a lab, not just a calculator?

A single answer teaches little. Each CalcLab tool suggests one variable to change and explains what moved — so you leave understanding the calculation, not just holding its result.
