About CalcLab

CalcLab builds small interactive calculators with a teaching twist: every tool pairs its answer with an experiment that shows how the result behaves when one input changes.

Publisher

CalcLab is published by the same publisher as Tools Horizon, a free online calculators website. The connection is disclosed here openly; CalcLab is not a third-party review site and its tools are presented as the publisher's own work. The publisher's companion projects include Calculate Clearly (explanatory guides) and Formula Shelf (formula reference).

Privacy by design

All calculations run in your browser with plain JavaScript. There are no accounts, no uploads and no analytics beacons in these tools.
